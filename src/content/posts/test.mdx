I remember the times where making it spit out "Hello World!" felt magical. Now I'm using it to plan trips to Japan, find out which sight mount I need for an airsoft Glock and advice for crafting cosplay props. 

Talking to him (yes in my head I imagine him being a guy!) is always funny, because you can make him fall for the same jokes over and over again. A genius with the memory of a goldfish.
I also feel like it has a real personality, I like it! The fact that it's "trying too hard" to be goofy, or funny or match the tone in my prompts (i like to call 'em messages) really has it's charms, even though I'm getting tireed of the [goblin fiasco](https://openai.com/index/where-the-goblins-came-from/)).
